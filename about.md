**Permeate** is an autonomous penetration testing platform. It discovers vulnerabilities, validates exploit chains, and generates remediation guidance — end to end, without manual intervention.

The name says it all: *find every way through, then close them all.*

This blog is where we publish research, document techniques, and share what we learn building offensive security systems that actually work at scale.
